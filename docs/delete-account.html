ShopWise Account and Data Deletion

Request account deletion without reinstalling the app.

Request deletion

ShopWise users can delete their account from the app under More → Delete Account. If you cannot access the app, email the Service Provider, Mohammad Adib Abtahi, to request deletion of your ShopWise account and associated data.

Use the email address associated with your ShopWise account when possible. Include your account email and, optionally, your shop name so the request can be identified. Never send your password, sign-in code, or payment credentials.

Email a deletion request

What will be deleted

After verifying your request, the Service Provider will delete your Firebase Authentication account and the associated ShopWise user profile, shop, products, batches, categories, invoices, customer/member records, and feedback where applicable.

Some information may be retained only when required for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, or resolving disputes. If information must be retained, the Service Provider will explain the reason and retention period where required by law.

Verification and processing

The Service Provider may contact you to verify that you own the account before deleting data. Requests will be processed within the timeframes required by applicable law. You can contact maabtahi818@gmail.com to ask about a request.
